## Full description (Google Play & App Store)

XMTO is the material take-off app for MEP engineers. Build plumbing and electrical take-offs on site,
price them with your company's rates, and share quotations, all from your phone.

**Take-offs in taps, not typing**
• Pick item → material → size → quantity from a structured MEP catalog
• Plumbing: pipes, fittings, valves, flanges, supports, jointing material
• Electrical: cables with cores, cable management, accessories
• Units come from the library, so every line is consistent

**One library for the whole team**
• Your company's rates and stock, shared with every field engineer
• Live stock: when a colleague uses the last of an item, your screen shows it as out of stock
• Admins control the library, and engineers always price from the latest rates

**Built for site work**
• Works offline and syncs when you're back in coverage
• Large touch targets for one-handed, gloved use
• Clear totals: material, labour, extras, discount and GST

**Quotations your clients can read**
• Professional PDF with your letterhead, GSTIN and terms
• Share by WhatsApp, email or print

**Team roles**
• Owner and Admin manage the library, team and approvals
• Field engineers make and submit their own estimates
• Viewers can see estimates but can't change them

Sign in with your email: you get a one-time code, with no password to remember.

XMTO is a product of XOITT Transformation, "Innovation in Action. Reliability by Design."
